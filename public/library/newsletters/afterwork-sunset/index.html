THE 80s & 90s RETURN

See You at Sunset.

Starting 21 October, join us at Ysabel Garden every Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday from 16:00 to 19:00. Sunset hours with selections from our menu, your favourite drinks, and music from the 80s and 90s.

There's probably someone you've been meaning to see. A friend you haven't caught up with in a while, or a colleague you'd enjoy spending a little more time with outside the office.

We'd love to welcome you both to Ysabel Garden.

Find something you like on the menu, enjoy a few drinks, and spend the sunset together with familiar songs from the 80s and 90s. The kind of music that brings back old memories and gives everyone something to talk about.

Whether you're stopping by for an hour or staying into the evening, we'd love for you to spend a little of that time with us.
