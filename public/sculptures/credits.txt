Ysabel Society — classical sculpture assets

Source: SMK — National Gallery of Denmark, Royal Cast Collection.
The 120 distinct sculptures in the current animation are selected from museum
records explicitly marked public domain. They include female and male figures,
goddesses, athletes, philosophers, and portraits from antiquity and later periods.
Public Domain Mark 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/

Every work's museum title, object ID, source record, original model URL, rights
reference, and optimized geometry hash are listed in collection.json in this folder.
The scene contains one instance of each work. Different rotations or materials
are not counted as additional sculptures.

Current collection adaptations: each scan simplified to 4,000 triangles,
centered, normalized, converted to Y-up, and oriented for display. The individual
geometries are packed into six binary volumes for efficient downloading.
